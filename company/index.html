Careers

Small team. Real projects.

Fully remote, deliberately small, close to the work. You’ll work with the founders on live projects and own real surface area from day one.

Open Operations · Full-time

General manager / Operations manager

Run delivery day to day: scope and quote each assignment, schedule the cost engineers, keep every project on time and own the client relationship after the sale.

You bring: operations or project management experience in construction, cost consulting or a professional-services firm, and you’re comfortable with process, numbers and clients.

Full role detailsHide details

The role

You’ll run Ladder’s operation in one location, end to end: the clients, the cost engineers and outside experts who deliver the work, and the R&D team building the tools behind it.

What you’ll do

  • Turn each incoming request into a scoped, priced assignment with a delivery date.
  • Staff every project with the right cost engineers and outside experts, and keep it on schedule.
  • Make sure every estimate is reviewed before it goes to the client.
  • Be the client’s day-to-day contact after the sale: kickoff, questions, delivery and follow-up.
  • Work with R&D: bring back what slows delivery and what clients ask for, and try new tools on live projects.
  • Build the local network of clients, experts and partners.

You bring

  • Experience running operations or projects in construction, cost consulting or a professional-services firm.
  • Comfort with numbers, schedules and scopes of work.
  • Clear communication with clients and with technical teams.
  • The judgment to run a small operation without someone checking every step.
