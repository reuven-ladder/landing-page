Questions

Before you ask for a quote.

More on how we work in Why Ladder.

Is this an AI estimate?
It’s AI-assisted and professionally reviewed. AI reads, counts and cross-checks the documents. Cost professionals price the work and review it before delivery. Every quantity links back to the sheet or spec section it came from, so you never have to take an AI’s word for a number.
What do I receive?
A cost estimate or quantity takeoff in Excel, organized by trade or package, with the basis of every line: where the quantity came from, how it was priced and what is still open. Plus a written report. See a sample estimate (US project).
How is AI used?
Specialist AI agents, one for each trade, read every drawing, specification and addendum in their part of the project, take off quantities and flag conflicts. A review step checks each trade against what it usually carries, and neighboring trades are reconciled so scope doesn’t fall between them. Our cost professionals lead the work.
Who is responsible for the estimate?
Ladder is responsible for the agreed work. Your proposal names the cost professional who reviews your project, with their experience and the scope of their review. If you need a signed report, we agree it as part of the assignment.
Can the AI get it wrong?
Yes, the same way a person can. That’s why every quantity says where it came from (printed, calculated, measured or judgment) and links back to the sheet or spec section. A cost professional reviews the items that move the number. We state what the analysis covered rather than promise it found everything.
What happens when the documents don’t answer a question?
It gets one of four treatments: a clarification for the design team, a stated assumption, an allowance or an exclusion. Each is listed with the estimate, so you can see what the number depends on.
How early can you start?
At concept. Early estimates matter most, because that’s when cost can still change the design. We work from whatever the project has, and the estimate gets more detailed as the design does.
Can you update the estimate when the design changes?
Yes. Quantities and assumptions stay linked to their sources, so a revision is assessed against the previous estimate: what changed and what it affects. Quantities and prices touched by the change are recalculated.
How is it priced?
A fixed fee per scope of work. We can start the conversation without drawings; to confirm a fixed fee, we may need to review the documents. We confirm the scope, fee and delivery schedule with you before starting, and we sign an NDA on request.
