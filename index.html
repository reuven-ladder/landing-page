How we work

From your documents to a reviewed estimate.

The basis of every line is documented, so your team can check a number, question an assumption or answer a clarification without redoing the estimate.

  1. 01 · Documents

    We start with your project documents.

    Drawings, specifications, addenda and revisions, reviewed together as one set. We use AI to analyze the documents.

  2. 02 · Scope and quantities

    Define the scope, then measure it.

    Our AI reads every sheet and specification section to build the scope: what is included, in which trade, and what is missing. Quantities are measured from the drawings and linked to their source. Our cost experts check both, and gaps become questions for the design team.

  3. 03 · Pricing and review

    Priced, anchored and reviewed.

    Rates are anchored in regional market data and in the costs of every project we have estimated. Each project adds to that knowledge, so our pricing gets sharper over time. Cost professionals then review rates, allowances and assumptions, and sign off the estimate before delivery.
